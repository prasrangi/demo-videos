# CRM Assist click-through

A click-through player for the recorded CRM Assist (Sales agent) demo, about three minutes of footage in nine steps. Each step opens with a card saying what comes next, then pauses on the prompt with a zoom and highlight box, and again on the reply. Continue (or Space or Enter) moves on. It starts with a title slide and ends with a summary slide. Idle waits are fast-forwarded, and the "Extra pauses" switch adds two more stops: the Salesforce sign-in window in step 2 and the second review proposal in step 9.

Recorded in a lab with generated sample data.

Open `index.html` through a web server or GitHub Pages. `walkthrough.mp4` must sit next to it.
