Keys and controls

Space Enter →
Continue. While the recording plays, skip ahead to the next stop.
P
Pause the recording while it plays, and resume it. While paused, Space also resumes and → skips ahead.
←
Back one stop, without playing.
+ − 0
Zoom in, zoom out, reset. You can also scroll over the recording, double-click a spot, and drag to move.
M
Sound on or off. With sound off, long dictation is fast forwarded.
A
Auto-play: move on by itself after each stop.
F H
Full screen. Hide or show the controls.
Home End
Title slide, recap slide.

The view zooms in by itself only while a prompt is entered. Results are always shown at full frame.
